# EXAMPLE DATA

Everything in this folder is **fake example data** used to test the template. It is not a real product, price or address.
The image is a generated placeholder (`node scripts/make-placeholders.mjs`). Replace it before publishing anything.

The product has two languages, English (the default) and German. The German texts are short, fake **AI-written drafts** (not reviewed by a native speaker), written to show a two-language site.

This product has fake **viewing settings** (`viewing` in product.json: an `example.invalid` address, Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays, 10:00 to 16:00 in one-hour visits, Europe/Vienna). The watch and bag examples have none, so they show the form without viewing requests.
